Our story

Three generations at the same wooden table

Golden Crust started in 1987 as a single oven in the back of a corner grocery. Today, the same starter culture we began with still leavens every sourdough we sell.

We mill nothing fancy and hide no shortcuts: flour, water, salt, time — plus early mornings and flour-dusted aprons. Everything is baked on site and sold the same day.

  • Slow-fermented doughs for better flavor
  • No preservatives, ever
  • Surplus donated to the community fridge nightly
